Free and open source · macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon and Intel

One account at a time is the problem.

Claude Desktop signs in to a single account. Lanes gives each one its own app, its own colored icon, and a place on the track.

Side by side

Every lane is a separate Claude with its own login, chats, settings and connectors. Open them all at once. Nothing leaks between them.

Usage at a glance

5-hour and weekly limits for every account, live, with reset countdowns. A pace marker shows if you're burning faster than the clock.

The lane with most room

One click, or ⌥⌘B from anywhere, opens the account with the most headroom left.

Always in sight

A menu bar gauge, a desktop widget, alerts at 80% and 95%, and a lanes command for your scripts.

Watch the track, not the clock.

The menu bar shows every lane's 5-hour usage next to the busiest percentage. It turns orange at 75% and red at 90%, so you see trouble before you hit it.

  • Alerts at 80% and 95%, and another the minute a limit resets
  • A week of history for every account
  • No account, no telemetry: usage is read with your own session

A new lane in ten seconds.

Name it, pick one of eight colors, and you get a fresh, empty Claude with a launcher in Spotlight and a matching tile in the Dock. The icon wears the lane's color, so you never sign in to the wrong account.

Claude's login link even lands on the right lane, however many are open.

Get on the track.

  1. Download Lanes.zip, open it, drag Lanes into Applications.
  2. Open it once, the long way. It isn't from the App Store: click Done, then Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Create a profile with New Profile, open it and sign in.
